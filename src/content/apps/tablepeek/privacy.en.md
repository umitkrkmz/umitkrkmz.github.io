## In short

* **No account, no telemetry, no ads, no analytics.** TablePeek has no developer server and **never connects to the internet.**
* Screen captures and images it reads are processed in memory only; **they are never saved or sent anywhere.**
* Text recognition uses the OCR built into Windows, on this PC only.
* All data stays **on your device**.

## Screen, pictures, and clipboard

* **Capture table:** When you press the shortcut or choose *Capture table*, TablePeek captures the screen, reads the table inside your selection in memory, and discards the image when you close the Studio window (in copy-directly mode it stays in memory until the next capture so the notification can still open it in the Studio).
* **Read from image / drag and drop:** The image file you pick or drop on the window is read-only; it is never modified, copied, or stored.
* **Image on clipboard:** The clipboard is read only when you choose *Image on clipboard* or press `Ctrl + V` in the TablePeek window.
* **Writing to the clipboard:** The table is written to the clipboard only when you copy it (or right after reading in *copy directly* mode).
* **Excel file:** *Save as .xlsx* writes only to the location you choose, when you trigger it.

## Data kept on your device

The app stores only what it needs to work:

* **Settings (`settings.json`):** Shortcut, language, reading and paste options, post-capture behaviour, and look preferences.
* **Error log (`error.log`):** Written only if something goes wrong, with technical details only; table contents and images are never logged.

In the Microsoft Store version this folder is managed by Windows for the app and **is removed when you uninstall it**.

## Purchases

TablePeek offers optional thank-you add-ons that unlock cosmetic extras only (colour themes, tray icon choices, and a supporter mark in About). Purchases are handled by Microsoft Store’s own dialog. Payment and licensing details are governed by Microsoft’s privacy statement and are not shared personally with the developer. TablePeek only asks Windows which add-ons are owned and what they cost.

## Links, sharing, and children

The privacy policy and issue links in Settings open in your default browser; *Open Windows language settings* opens the Windows Settings app only. Because the developer collects no data, nothing is sold or shared with third parties. TablePeek is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
