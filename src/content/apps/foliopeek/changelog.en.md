## v1.2.0 — 2026-11-24

* **My notes (adding quotes is Pro):** Right-click selected text or a highlight and choose *Save to my notes* to collect quotes with their file and page in one local notebook (`Ctrl + Shift + N`, *Tools* menu, or the home screen card). Grouped by document, searchable, with room for your own comment under each quote; **Go to page** opens the PDF and makes the quote glow for a few seconds. Export to Markdown (`.md`) or TXT (`.txt`). Reading, opening, deleting, and exporting saved notes are always free, and notes stay strictly on this PC.
* **Search the web (free):** Right-click selected text → *Search Bing for "…"* (choose Bing, Google, or DuckDuckGo in *Settings → Reading*). Your default browser runs the search; FolioPeek itself still never goes online.
* **Highlight right-click menu:** Right-clicking a highlight no longer deletes it immediately—it opens a menu with *Delete highlight* and *Save to my notes*.
* **One-time rating prompt:** After a few jobs succeed, the Microsoft Store rating window may appear once and never asks again.

## v1.1.0 — 2026-11-24

### New print window (`Ctrl + P`, free)

* **Live preview:** What you see in the preview is what lands on paper; scrolls, zooms, and shows two sheets side by side when they fit.
* **Printer state & active page:** Shows the printer’s live status (*ready*, *offline*, *needs attention*), and *this page* follows the page currently visible in the preview.
* **Automatic orientation & colour:** Lays out each page in its own orientation, with copies, collation, and colour or black-and-white options right at hand.
* **Simple two-sided printing:** Pick *Like a book* or *Like a calendar*, and FolioPeek automatically chooses the long or short flip edge.
* **More settings:** Paper size and type, print quality, 1–2–4–6–9 pages per sheet, actual size / fit to paper / custom scale, margins, notes, reverse order, and date/time stamps on paper.
* **Remembered per printer:** Choices are saved per printer. The built-in Windows print dialog remains one shortcut away via `Ctrl + Shift + P`.

## v1.0.1 — 2026-11-23

* **Help & support link:** *Help → Help and report a problem* now opens the login-free [help and support page](https://github.com/umitkrkmz/FolioPeek/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
* **Rate this app:** Added under the *Help* menu and *Settings → About*; opens the rating page in Microsoft Store (Microsoft Store connects, while FolioPeek itself still never goes online).
* **FolioPeek Pro:** Available in Microsoft Store as a one-time purchase; use *Restore purchase* on another PC or after reinstalling.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-20

First release in the Microsoft Store.

### Free features

* Fast tabbed reading, bookmarks, `Ctrl + F` search; **Original**, **Dark**, and **Sepia** reading modes.
* Highlights, freehand ink, and text notes saved as real PDF annotations; every step can be undone with `Ctrl + Z`.
* Form filling; drawn, typed, or image-based signatures; today’s date and short text stamps.
* Two ways to merge: a classic file list and a visual merge board where you drag pages and wire the order with cables (preserving bookmarks, links, forms, and notes).
* Page organization: rotate, reorder, delete, extract, and insert pages from other PDFs.
* Images to PDF and export PDF pages as PNG / JPEG at 150 or 300 DPI.
* Document properties (`Ctrl + D`), printing, light and dark themes, English and Turkish interface.

### Pro (one-time purchase)

* Verified permanent redaction.
* Offline text recognition (OCR) and searchable PDF generation powered by Windows OCR.
* Password protection (AES-128), metadata cleanup, and metadata editing.
* Watermarks, page numbers, headers, and footers.

### Privacy

* Never goes online; declares no network capability, with no account, ads, or telemetry.
