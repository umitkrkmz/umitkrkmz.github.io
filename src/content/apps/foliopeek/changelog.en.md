## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-20

First release in the Microsoft Store.

### Free features

* Fast tabbed reading, bookmarks, `Ctrl + F` search; **Original**, **Dark**, and **Sepia** reading modes.
* Highlights, freehand ink, and text notes saved as real PDF annotations; every step can be undone with `Ctrl + Z`.
* Form filling; drawn, typed, or image-based signatures; today’s date and short text stamps.
* Two ways to merge: a classic file list and a visual merge board where you drag pages and wire the order with cables (preserving bookmarks, links, forms, and notes).
* Page organization: rotate, reorder, delete, extract, and insert pages from other PDFs.
* Images to PDF and export PDF pages as PNG / JPEG at 150 or 300 DPI.
* Document properties (`Ctrl + D`), printing, light and dark themes, English and Turkish interface.

### Pro (one-time purchase)

* Verified permanent redaction.
* Offline text recognition (OCR) and searchable PDF generation powered by Windows OCR.
* Password protection (AES-128), metadata cleanup, and metadata editing.
* Watermarks, page numbers, headers, and footers.

### Privacy

* Never goes online; declares no network capability, with no account, ads, or telemetry.
